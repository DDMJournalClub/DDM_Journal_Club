## 🇨🇳 中文版本

### 关于 DDMJC

DDMJC (Diffusion Decision Model Journal Club) 是一个由 **Drift-Diffusion Model (DDM)** 兴趣小组发起的线上文献分享与交流活动。

我们致力于通过线上方式与国内外学生和同行交流学习 DDM 及相关模型。活动主要关注：

- 扩散决策模型的基础知识及相关研究
- DDM 在决策任务中的应用、代码实现及数学原理
- 认知建模和心理学方法的相关讨论

## 🇬🇧 English Version

### About DDMJC

DDMJC (Diffusion Decision Model Journal Club) is an online literature sharing and exchange event initiated by the **Drift-Diffusion Model (DDM)** interest group.

Our purpose is to communicate and learn about DDM and related models with students and colleagues both domestically and internationally through online means. The event primarily focuses on:

- The basic knowledge of the drift-diffusion model and related research
- The application of DDM in decision-making tasks, code implementation, and mathematical principles
- Discussions on cognitive modeling and psychological methods
