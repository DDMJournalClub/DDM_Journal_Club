完全睡眠剥夺后抑制控制任务间受损的共性和差异性：基于漂移扩散模型的证据

Commonality and Diversity of Impairments in Inhibitory Tasks After Total Sleep Deprivation: Evidence from Drift Diffusion Model

分享嘉宾

曹艺轩
华南师范大学心理学院博士研究生，导师为马宁教授。主要研究方向：睡眠缺失对认知功能的影响。

报告简介

This study investigated the effects of total sleep deprivation on two key sub-components of inhibitory control—interference inhibition and response inhibition—and examined whether sleep deprivation affects these components through shared or distinct cognitive mechanisms.
